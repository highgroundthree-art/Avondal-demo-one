Five-minute run sheet
  1. Home: point out the new Shop Parts tab and the Shop Parts button next to Schedule a Service.
  2. Shop: real J&M parts, photos and retail prices. Search "air filter", then pick Honda under Make. Point out the fitment flags.
  3. Hand Dennis the device. He adds two or three parts and opens the cart.
  4. Show the other way to buy: tap Find a part and type "air filter for my Honda TRX420". Add a part from the chat.
  5. At checkout, show both choices: Ship to my address or Ship to the shop (pickup or install). Dennis gets a ticket either way.
  6. Checkout as a customer: he types his real email. Use "Fill demo details" for the rest. The order ships straight to the customer.
  7. On the confirmation page, walk through what happens behind the scenes and the human review step.
  8. Have him open his inbox. The real receipt arrives from the shop's Gmail.
  9. Tap Help & Returns, then "Start a return". He picks the part and a reason and gets a return number plus a follow-up email.
  10. Turn on Mechanic order below and run the same flow again. This time it ships to the workshop with a work order number.
