Presenter panel

Five-minute run sheet
  1. Home: point out the new Shop Parts tab and the Shop Parts button next to Schedule a Service.
  2. Shop: search "brake", then choose 1981 Honda CR250R under "Shopping for". Point out the fitment flags.
  3. Hand Dennis the device. He adds two or three parts and opens the cart.
  4. Checkout as a customer: he types his real email. Use "Fill demo details" for the rest. The order ships straight to the customer.
  5. On the confirmation page, walk through what happens behind the scenes and the human review step.
  6. Have him open his inbox. The real receipt arrives from the shop's Gmail.
  7. Tap Help & Returns, then "Start a return". He picks the part and a reason and gets a return number plus a follow-up email.
  8. Turn on Mechanic order below and run the same flow again. This time it ships to the workshop with a work order number.
